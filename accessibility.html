Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 15 October 2026

We want every business owner to be able to use Mortgage AI, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard only, screen magnification or other assistive technology. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA.

What we do

  • Every form field has a visible label.
  • The site and app work in English, Spanish, French, Arabic and Hebrew, with right-to-left layout for Arabic and Hebrew.
  • Pages can be zoomed and work on small phone screens.
  • We use plain language and avoid time limits on the request form.

Known limits

  • This Accessibility Statement, the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use are currently in English only.
  • The site and app have not yet been reviewed by an independent accessibility auditor. We plan to do this and fix what it finds.

Tell us about a problem

If something is hard to use, or you need information in another format, email us at mortgageloneai@gmail.com. Tell us the page and what happened. We aim to reply within 5 business days, and we can also take your funding request by email.
