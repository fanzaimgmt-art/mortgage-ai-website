Your questions, answered

Is it really free?
Yes. Sending a request costs you nothing. If you work with our licensed funding partner, they may pay Mortgage AI a referral fee. The cost of any funding is set by the partner, so ask for the full cost before you agree.
Will this affect my credit score?
Sending a request won't, because Mortgage AI does not pull credit. If you choose to move forward with the funding partner, any credit check is done by the partner, with your authorization.
Will I get a flood of calls?
That's exactly what we're built to avoid. We don't sell or resell your number to a network of lenders. Your request goes to one licensed funding partner, and that partner is the one who contacts you. You can reply STOP to texts at any time.
Are you a lender?
No. Mortgage AI is a technology and referral platform. We pass your request to one licensed funding partner, who contacts you and presents any offer. We don't lend money, set terms or make approval decisions, and we don't pull credit. Availability varies by state.
What kinds of funding can I ask about?
Business-purpose funding only: working capital, equipment financing and revenue-based financing. Availability and terms vary by state and by partner.
